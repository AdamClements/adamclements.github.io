Chapter One

Perfect Day

for Mac, Windows, Linux and Android

Perfect Day is a calm, keyboard-first place to write novels and screenplays. The screen breaks lines and pages exactly where the printed page will, so what you write is already the book: chapters open with their capitals dropped, scripts sit in their proper margins, and nothing on the screen asks for your attention but the words.

perfectdaywriter.com

Chapter Two

Glance

a home screen for Android

Glance puts today’s screen time front and centre, over a hand-drawn garden that follows your week. Use your phone less than you used to and the garden grows; use it more and it dies back. Each hour past the day’s aim costs a plant, and a time-waster has to cut one down before it will open.

The weather is today and the season is the trend. Robins and bees come to a garden that’s doing well, and at night, fireflies.

Chapter Three

FreeCell

for Android, and in the browser

The classic card game, made because the one on my phone was full of adverts. No adverts, no internet, no tracking: the numbered deals everyone knows, big clear cards with classic court pictures, and undo as far back as you like.

Chapter Four

Tabula Rasa

a game, still being written

A real-time strategy game in which every unit runs a virtual machine of its own, and you win by writing the code that coordinates them. Under it sit compilers and interpreters for custom machine code, radio between the units, and a 3D engine made from the ground up.
