Perfect Dayfor Mac, Windows, Linux and Android

A writing app for novels and screenplays. I wanted to write in something that already looked like the finished book, so Perfect Day sets the page while you type: real page breaks, dropped capitals, a script’s margins where a script’s margins go. When you want to read it back, you turn the page and it’s a book.

Glancea home screen for Android

Android’s screen time settings were too easy to wave away, so I replaced the home screen. Glance shows how long you’ve spent on your phone today over a garden drawn side-on. It grows slowly on the days you leave the phone alone and loses a plant for every hour you go over, and the apps you’ve marked as time-wasters won’t open until you’ve cut something down. It turns out to be much harder to ignore a hollyhock than a notification.

FreeCellfor Android

Every FreeCell I tried put a full-screen advert between games. This one has no adverts and doesn’t even ask for the internet. It deals the 32,000 numbered games, so game 1234 is the same one here as anywhere else, and undo goes all the way back to the deal.

Tabula Rasaa game, unfinished

A strategy game where you don’t command your units, you program them. Each one runs its own small virtual machine and talks to the others by radio, so winning comes down to writing code that gets them to work together. Underneath are a compiler and interpreter for its machine code and a 3D engine written from scratch. The teaser site is the terminal you start at.
