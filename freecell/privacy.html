FreeCell – No Ads: privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026.

FreeCell – No Ads (the Android app uk.adamclements.freecell) does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

  • The app has no internet access: Android doesn't give it permission to use the network.
  • It has no adverts, analytics, crash reporting or accounts.
  • Your saved game and statistics are kept only in the app's own storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them. Android's own device backup may include them if you have backup turned on; that is governed by Google's policy, not this app.
  • The only permission it asks for is vibration, for a short buzz when a move isn't allowed.
