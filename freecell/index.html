FreeCell – No Ads

Classic FreeCell solitaire. No ads, no internet, no tracking. Just cards.

No trackingYour games and scores never leave your phone.

Made for one hand on a phone

  • Tap a card to send it to the best place, or drag cards and whole runs.
  • Undo as far back as you like; hold Undo to rewind.
  • Hints play the move for you to see.
  • Once the game is won, the rest of the cards go home by themselves.
  • The classic 32,000 numbered deals, so game #11982 means the same here as anywhere.
  • Timer, moves and a score; your best score, best time, fewest moves and streaks are kept on your phone.
  • Your game is saved the moment you leave, and the clock waits for you.

Why

I liked the FreeCell game on my phone and hated its adverts, so I made my own. It does the one thing and gets out of the way.
