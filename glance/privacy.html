Glance: Screen Time Garden: privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026.

Glance: Screen Time Garden (the Android app uk.adamclements.glance) is a home screen. This page says what it reads, what it keeps and what it sends.

Your screen time stays on your phone

  • Glance reads how long you've used each app from Android's usage access, which you turn on in set-up. It uses this to show today's time and to grow the garden.
  • Your screen time, the apps you have and the apps you use are kept only in the app's own storage on your phone. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them. Android's own device backup may include them if you have backup turned on; that is governed by Google's policy, not this app.

The garden gate (accessibility service)

  • If you turn it on, Glance uses an Android accessibility service so the garden can ask first when a time-waster opens from a notification, a link or recent apps.
  • It only hears which app came to the front. It can't read what's on the screen, and it keeps nothing and sends nothing.
  • You can turn it off at any time in Android's Accessibility settings.

Anonymous crash reports and counts

To find bugs and see which parts of Glance get used, it sends anonymous data to PostHog, on its servers in the EU (eu.i.posthog.com):

  • Crash reports: the error and where in Glance's code it happened.
  • Counts of what happens in Glance itself: set-up steps, the gate being shown and whether a plant was cut or left (and what kind of plant), the app list being opened, an app being moved between groups (never which app), apps being put on or taken off the home screen, today's breakdown being opened, a seed being sown (and what flower), and once a day how many apps are in each group and on the home screen (numbers only).
  • About the phone: the app's version, the Android version and its SDK level.

Each install has a random ID, made on the phone, so counts from one phone can be told apart. It isn't linked to you, your Google account or your phone's own identifiers, and PostHog keeps no profile of you. Glance never sends app names, package names or screen-time figures.

What Glance doesn't do

  • No adverts, no accounts, no sign-in.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Permissions

  • Usage access: to read your screen time.
  • Accessibility (optional): to know when a time-waster comes to the front.
  • Internet: only for the anonymous crash reports and counts above.
  • Vibration: a short buzz when a plant is cut down.
